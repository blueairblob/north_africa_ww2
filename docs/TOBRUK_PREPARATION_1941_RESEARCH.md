# Between Battleaxe and Crusader: research for a linked trial

**Scope:** 18 June–31 December 1941. This is evidence for designing linked chapters, not a validated opening deployment. Four linked trial chapters now cover **18 June–31 December**. No automatic historical outcome is installed. The independent Crusader battle remains a separate historical start.

## What the sources establish

| Period | Evidence | Consequence for an open campaign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June–July | [UK official history, volume II, chapter XIII](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-13.html) describes stalemate after Battleaxe, an argument over the timing of the next Commonwealth offensive, tank repair and training needs, and Axis supply difficulties. | Both commanders need meaningful preparation choices. Neither a compulsory ceasefire nor a fixed surrender or capture follows from the date. |
| July–October | The same chapter says 22nd Armoured Brigade's convoy arrived on **4 October**, later than hoped, and that it needed desert preparation. It records 5th Light Division's redesignation as **21st Panzer Division on 1 August**. | Do not spawn new German tanks just because the calendar advances. The source attributes the rise in serviceable tanks of the two German panzer divisions, from roughly 180 to roughly 250, mainly to **repair**, not shipments. A name change must preserve formation identity in a running campaign. |
| August–October | The [UK official history's chronology](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-Chronology.html) gives **19–29 August** for the first stage of Australian relief and **19–27 September** for the second. [Auchinleck's despatch, section 29](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/LondonGazette/37695.pdf), gives the remaining relief windows as **18–28 September** and **12–26 October**, the 2/13th Battalion's continued presence and Scobie's assumption of Tobruk command on **22 October**. The [Australian War Memorial's siege account](https://www.awm.gov.au/articles/encyclopedia/tobruk) confirms that most of the 9th Division was relieved but 2/13th remained. | The port's ownership and sea access must be checked before any garrison rotation. In a played campaign where Tobruk fell, became accessible by land, or lacks the listed Australian units, the event needs a conditional alternative. Do not withdraw the entire 9th Division unconditionally. |
| October–November | [UK official history, volume II, chapter XV](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-15.html) says Auchinleck preferred a November offensive to an uncertain early-October one, because armour, maintenance resources and training needed time. The first aim was to retake Cyrenaica by defeating Axis armour. | The Commonwealth briefing should state the operational aim and the cost of premature attack. Preparation must affect serviceability, stocks or readiness in the game, rather than merely add empty turns. |
| November | The [Australian War Memorial's siege account](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/E84833) identifies the 70th Division as the Tobruk defender when Crusader began. | Crusader's independent historical start must remain separate from a played bridge. A campaign transition inherits its actual line, holdings and losses. |
| November–December | [Auchinleck's Crusader despatch and operation instruction, London Gazette 38177](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/LondonGazette/38177.pdf), lists the planned Eighth Army force, including XXX and XIII Corps, the New Zealand and South African divisions and army tank brigades. | The linked chapter adds missing formations with new IDs; the official formation list does not validate their game strength, readiness day or map entry point. |

## Mechanics required before admitting the bridge

1. **Refit and serviceability.** Track damaged versus operational tanks and repair capacity. The present step, reinforcement and supply rules cannot distinguish repaired tanks from newly shipped vehicles. The official history's Axis tank figures should be a calibration target, not a scripted grant.
2. **Dated, conditional garrison relief.** The August–September trial schedules a Polish brigade entry and 18th Australian Brigade sea relief in the turn containing 29 August. The September–November chapter adds 70th Division HQ on 22 October and three brigade counters on 26 October as a coarse proxy for the progressive September–October sea exchange. Australian brigade withdrawals on 26 October occur only near friendly-held Tobruk; 20th Brigade instead leaves a 2/13th Battalion remnant. A lost port sends incoming British or Polish units to the Commonwealth base, and leaves Australian formations elsewhere in play. The Polish brigade's approximately 5,000 men, the British brigade strengths and the 2/13th's 800 men are model estimates. The 70th Division brigades are identified in [the official British medical history's Tobruk garrison list](https://www.generalstaff.org/WW2/Hist_UK/ArmyMedicalServicesCampVol1.pdf). The current model does not distinguish embarkation capacity or a failed convoy; no automatic teleport into an Axis-held Tobruk occurs.
3. **Preparation choices.** Permit either side to attack earlier, rest, accumulate supply or manoeuvre. A long quiet interval needs visible strategic consequences and the existing F6 advance should stop when contact, arrivals, changed control or supply trouble demands orders.
4. **Continuous scenario data.** The bridge begins **18 June 1941** and now reaches **31 December** through a linked Crusader trial. The checker and handoff reject date gaps. The linked Crusader chapter inherits the played 17 November state, whereas the independent Crusader opening has a different roster and IDs. The current HQ label inherited from 1940 still says Western Desert Force, although it became XIII Corps at the start of 1941; this needs a dated redesignation across earlier chapters. A remnant name is permitted only when a chapter explicitly lists it as the same formation's alternate name.
5. **Historical audit.** Verify actual October-November unit rosters, relief dates, stock flows, repaired tank availability and air strength against official histories or war diaries. The sources above do not establish exact 10 km placements, unit-level strengths or per-turn supply.

**Design inference:** the June–July stalemate, August–September relief and September–November preparation boundaries are game structure, not a claim that fighting stopped on those dates. The place-holding orders are design interpretations; the cited sources do not give those exact orders. The 22nd Armoured Brigade becomes an active counter on **1 November** as a readiness estimate; [the UK official history](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-13.html) gives its convoy arrival on 4 October and the need for desert preparation, not an exact active date or the game counter's 80 tanks. [UK official history, volume I, chapter XV](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-I/UK-Med-I-15.html) dates the Western Desert Force's XIII Corps title to 1 January 1941.

The **18 November–31 December linked Crusader trial** uses 22 two-day turns. Its missions require Commonwealth control of Sidi Rezegh and Belhamed and an enemy-free map corridor to Tobruk; Axis orders require blocking the corridor and holding Bardia. These are explicit game interpretations of the offensive, not verbatim operational orders. The corridor omits Ed Duda and is not an exact road. New Zealand and South African formation counters and army tank brigades become available on 18 November as a game convention; their strengths and Mersa Matruh entry points are estimates. The 32nd Army Tank Brigade enters Tobruk only while it remains friendly held. The chapter does not apply the independent battle's scripted losses or withdrawals to a counterfactual campaign.

The [Australian War Memorial timeline](https://www.awm.gov.au/learn/schools/resources/tobruk-diaries/timeline) dates evacuation of the last Australian battalion to **10 December**. The linked chapter now withdraws the 2/13th Battalion then only if the October relief left that specific remnant near Commonwealth-held Tobruk. If the 20th Brigade was not relieved in the played campaign, the December event does not remove it.
