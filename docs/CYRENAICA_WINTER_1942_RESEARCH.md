# Cyrenaica winter and January offensive, 1942

**Status:** linked campaign research trial through 8 February 1942, not a verified historical standalone start.

## Dated evidence

- [Major General Alfred Toppe, *Desert Warfare: German Experiences in World War II*, chapter II, section 7e](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USA/CSI/CSI-Desert/toppe.asp.html) describes the Axis reaching the Marada–Mersa Brega line by 10 January, its limited counterattack beginning on 21 January, and the historical capture of Agedabia on 22 January, Benghazi on 30 January and Derna on 4 February. Fuel and troop condition prevented an immediate attack on Tobruk; the advance halted by 7 February. This is a German retrospective account. The chapter dates are game boundaries; none of these historical captures are forced.
- The [Australian War Memorial's Tobruk timeline](https://www.awm.gov.au/learn/schools/resources/tobruk-diaries/timeline) dates the last Australian battalion's evacuation to 10 December 1941. The linked Crusader chapter withdraws unit 351 on that date only if it is still the 2/13th Battalion remnant near Commonwealth-held Tobruk. An unrelieved 20th Brigade remains in the played campaign.
- Toppe identifies the 90th Light Division's coastal role and the Afrika Korps' attempted envelopment in the January operation. The existing counters do not reliably distinguish the exact 1942 unit strengths, equipment or reorganization. The trial therefore does not add a fresh division at full strength or restore losses by fiat.

## Game interpretation

The 1–20 January chapter uses ten two-day turns for consolidation. The 21 January–8 February chapter uses nineteen daily turns for the counteroffensive and pursuit. Both carry the preceding chapter's surviving roster, location, control, fortifications and stocks. The new High Command aims require the Commonwealth to hold Gazala, Tobruk and the Egyptian approach for a tactical defensive success, and the Axis to hold Agedabia and Benghazi for a major advance. The requirements use actual end-of-chapter occupation, so stale ownership from an earlier chapter cannot alone complete them. If the player's 1941 course has left a different line, the commanders can still fight over these aims; the model does not relocate them to the historical line.

The current trial has major historical gaps: tank repair and serviceability, Axis shipping and fuel stocks, exact 1942 orders of battle, deception before 21 January, battlefield withdrawals, minefields and the prepared Gazala positions. These need source-led rules and validation before the chapter can be described as a simulation of the historical winter campaign. The following Gazala preparation chapter covers 9 February through 25 May without skipping strategic time.
