# Bill of materials: pictures, effects and sounds

Every picture, visual effect and sound the game uses or is known to need,
where each comes from now, and what is still wanted. Checked against the
code on 26 October 2026; the art inventory remains in progress.

How to read the tables:

- **Source.** *Owner's AI picture*: generated by an AI image tool for this game
  and given by the owner, under no copyright (`docs/SOURCES.md`, Art).
  *Drawn set*: drawn by `python -m countergen` from shapes in
  `countergen/__init__.py`. *Drawn in code*: drawn by pygame each time it is
  shown, in `screen/draw.py` or `screen/pictures.py`. *Generated*: made by a
  tool in this repository from public data. *Synthesised*: sound made from sums
  in `screen/sound.py`; there are no sound files.
- **Status.** *In use*: done, nothing needed. *Stopgap*: in use, but to be
  replaced. *Missing*: the game shows a plainer fallback. *Not built*:
  described in the docs, nothing on the screen. *Future*: belongs to the web
  version, not to be started unasked (`PLAN.md`).
- **Priority** (pictures only, as in `docs/COUNTERS.md`, "Art still required"): 1 missing from the
  game; 2 the wrong subject; 3 to be redrawn one to a file, to the brief.

## 1. Counter pictures

Eighteen: six kinds for each of three nations. Each has a brief with an exact
prompt, reference images and acceptance checks: `art/art_required_<nation>_<kind>.md`.
The screen takes `art/counters/artist/<nation>_<kind>.png` if it exists, else
the drawn set's `art/counters/<nation>_<kind>.png`. Units with an explicit
`counter_art` choice first use `art/counters/variants/<nation>_<counter_art>.png`.

Every picture must be a 480 × 288 PNG with a real alpha channel, facing right,
its wheels, tracks or feet on the line at y = 248, within 410 × 214 and
centred, and readable at 43 × 26 pixels (the counter at the opening zoom).
`tests/test_counters.py` checks the size and placement.

"Units" is how many of Crusader's 91 units show the picture.
`docs/COUNTER_AUDIT.md` enumerates all 91 by their selected sprite and
separates type-level evidence from unit-inventory checks still required.

Armour variants for the other four brigades: `art/counters/variants/cw_stuart.png`
for 4th Armoured Brigade; original `cw_a13.png` for 7th Armoured Brigade;
`cw_matilda.png` for 1st and 32nd Army Tank Brigades. These are representative
silhouettes: 7th Armoured also had Crusaders and A10s, both army-tank brigades
also operated other types, and 22nd Armoured later included Stuarts.
The Matilda variant is a new original Matilda II painting, replacing the
first sheet cut. Its muted three-tone Caunter-inspired paint is illustrative,
not an identification of either brigade's specific vehicles or scheme.
Neither a one-sprite-per-brigade portrayal nor the current scenario data
can fully identify every tank within a mixed formation. `cw_mkvi_light.png`
shows a representative Mark VI light tank of New Zealand Divisional Cavalry
(unit 335), distinct from the wheeled-car default for unit 305. The NZ force
also had Bren carriers, represented by the original but now unassigned
`cw_carrier.png`. Unit 335's `cars: 30` scenario field remains an unverified
rules/strength abstraction, labelled scout vehicles in the screen, not a
literal count of either light tanks or armoured cars.

The 8th and 9th Bersaglieri (units 152 and 163) use an original
`it_bersaglieri_motorised.png` variant: an open, unmarked lorry with
feathered troops, instead of the covered lorry used by the 65th and 66th.
The precise vehicle allocation and motorcycle component remain unknown;
the riders identify the troops, not an exact regiment equipment return.

Italian artillery units 143 and 173 use an original `it_obice10017.png`
howitzer variant in place of the shared 75/27 modello 06 image. Savona's
1941 regimental history lists a 100/17 group along with 105/28, 75/27 and
65/17 guns; Brescia's dated 1 November return lists both 100/17 and
75/27-11 groups. The art shows one documented type in each mixed unit,
while each scenario's 24 guns remain an abstract strength.

Italian 7th Bersaglieri (unit 183) uses an original infantry variant
`art/counters/variants/it_bersaglieri.png` with dark feathered M33 helmets,
not the eleven other foot units' plain-helmeted `it_foot.png`. The 8th and
9th Bersaglieri are motorised scenario units; their separate open-lorry
variant now distinguishes their troops from ordinary motorised infantry.

Two German gun variants complete the first subject correction:
`de_flak88.png` (deployed 8.8 cm Flak, Halfaya garrison, unit 104) and
`de_antitank.png` (new original Panzerjäger I on a Panzer I chassis,
representing the self-propelled 605 element of grouped battalions 39/605,
unit 125). The picture does not depict Battalion 39's towed guns or
prove the group's exact number and command assignment. Field/mixed artillery units 106, 113, 123 and 134
show the painted default leFH 18.

Currently: all 18 nation/kind defaults have individual painted images,
including the Commonwealth and German infantry that replaced sheet cuts.
Additional armour, NZ Mark VI, Bersaglieri and German gun variants supply
formation-specific representative subjects. The three recon types are
representative pictures: exact model mixes, RECAM's reported photograph
and full vehicle mix, and the Halfaya/Panzerjäger gun groups remain
unverified. See `docs/COUNTERS.md`
for unresolved historic types.

Open on the Axis HQ cars: their pennants are black and tricolour where the briefs
ask for red, and which car the Italian one shows was not checked.

## 5. The playback

All drawn in code (`Painter.stage`, scenes from `screen/replay.py`), all in use.

| Effect | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Units moving hex by hex; a road column drawn small until it arrives | the movement, grouped so that each group is in view |
| A red ring round the battle hex | where the battle is |
| A line from striker to target | who strikes whom |
| White and yellow border on the striking counter | the one firing |
| Burning: the struck counter flashes yellow and red, with a glow on the ground, and shakes | it is being hit |
| Green border | it held its ground |
| Rings and sparks, then gone | a unit destroyed |
| A white flag going up | a unit surrendering |
| A band across the top with the scene's title | what is being shown |
| The rattle meter: forty blocks lit for as long as the rattle | the damage, seen as well as heard |
| Units falling back, then the victors following | the outcome, cause before effect |

## 8. For the web version later

Future, not to be started unasked (`PLAN.md`, "Simple mode" and "A living
map"). Listed so the art can be planned with it in mind.

- Simple mode's division marker and its one card.

  behind a moving formation, a moving sandstorm haze, blowing sand, heat
  shimmer.

  is near a phone browser's limit (the figure is from memory).
