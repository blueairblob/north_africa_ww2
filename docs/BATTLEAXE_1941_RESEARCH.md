# Halfaya and Battleaxe, May–June 1941

**Status, 8 November 2026:** Three linked research chapters extend the open campaign from **18 May through 17 June 1941**. They are playable continuations from the preceding Brevity trial, not independently verified historical starts. Each handoff keeps the actual ownership, unit losses, fortifications and supply stocks.

The [UK official history, *The Mediterranean and Middle East*, volume II, chapter VIII](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-8.html) dates the German attack on Halfaya Pass to **26–27 May**. It records Wavell's Battleaxe orders of **28 May**: first defeat the Axis frontier force and secure the Bardia–Sollum–Capuzzo area, then deal with the force around Tobruk and El Adem, then exploit west. It says that *Tiger* tanks needed unloading, desert modification and training, setting **15 June** for the offensive. The battle ran through **17 June** and the actual Commonwealth attack failed. None of those outcomes is scripted into a played campaign.

## Research implementation

- **18–27 May:** Five two-day turns give both sides a clear Halfaya objective. The Axis may attack or defend it; Commonwealth forces may keep it or retake it. A dated mission does not set its owner or order a retreat.
- **28 May–14 June:** Nine two-day turns allow assembly, supply and defence. The model adds a 15th Panzer frontier HQ and 8th Panzer Regiment, 4th Indian Division HQ, 11th Indian Brigade, artillery and two Commonwealth armoured reinforcement groups on **1 June**. This is an estimated *game entry date*, not the historical date each unit reached Africa or the frontier. They enter at a friendly Bardia or Mersa Matruh; if enemy-held, the existing arrival rule uses a rear base.
- **15–17 June:** Three daily turns score actual occupation of Sollum and Fort Capuzzo for the Commonwealth and Halfaya Pass and Bardia for the Axis. Holding Halfaya offers the Commonwealth a separate optional commendation. These are map-scale frontier aims; the whole historical Battleaxe plan would also need the Tobruk and El Adem phase, contingent on what happens at the frontier.

The [official history](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-8.html) gives roughly **90 cruiser and 100 infantry tanks** at Battleaxe's start and approximately **100 tanks** in the 8th Panzer Regiment. The added 60 cruiser and 75 infantry tanks are **model estimates** that, together with the Brevity groups if they survive, approach the historical Commonwealth totals. They are separate new groups: the game does not restore a destroyed earlier formation or reset its casualties. The 11th Indian Brigade's 3,000 men, divisional artillery's 24 guns, exact entry hexes and 1 June date are estimates. The generic combat system cannot yet reproduce tank breakdowns, concealed anti-tank guns, the Hafid ridge position, minefields, transport shortages or the differing Axis and Commonwealth recovery rates. No convoy unloading, training or unit serviceability pool exists yet; the delay before the modelled 1 June arrivals is an abstraction.

The copied scenario owners and placements are only checker placeholders.
