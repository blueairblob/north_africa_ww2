# Frontier preparation and Brevity, May 1941

**Status, 8 November 2026:** Two linked research chapters continue the played campaign from 5 through 17 May 1941. These are open-play intervals, not verified independent historical starts. They preserve the actual position, losses, port stocks and formations after 4 May.

The [UK official history, *The Mediterranean and Middle East*, volume II, chapter VIII](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-8.html) describes Wavell's order for Brevity: drive Axis forces from Sollum and Capuzzo, inflict losses and exploit toward Tobruk only as supply and the safety of the force permitted. It describes three groups: the desert-flank 7th Armoured Brigade Group with 29 cruiser tanks, 22nd Guards Brigade Group with 4th Royal Tank Regiment's 24 infantry tanks, and a coast group built mainly from 2nd Rifle Brigade and 8th Field Regiment. The operation began on **15 May**; an Axis counterattack and British withdrawal followed on **16 May**. The game treats these as background to its orders, not events it must reproduce. A player may already hold the frontier, may have lost Tobruk or may move elsewhere.

## Implemented research abstraction

- **5–14 May:** Five two-day preparation turns let both sides maneuver, resupply, recover and defend their played holdings. Four Commonwealth groups enter from Mersa Matruh on 5 May in the model. That date and rear entry are estimates to allow movement before the frontier operation; it is not a claim that all four historical groups assembled there on that date. If the base is enemy-held, the normal arrival rule falls back to another friendly base rather than creating units in enemy territory.
- **15–17 May:** Three daily turns ask the Commonwealth to occupy Sollum and Fort Capuzzo at the end of the chapter. The Axis is ordered to hold Halfaya Pass and Bardia. These are playable operational aims. A Commonwealth optional commendation recognises occupying and retaining Halfaya Pass at the debrief. It has no effect on victory.
- The historical 29 cruiser and 24 infantry tanks inform the two tank counters. The 3,000 men assigned to the Guards group, 1,800 to Coast Group, 5 May model arrival and exact map entry are **game estimates**. These groups are separate campaign formations and do not revive any 7th Armoured counters previously withdrawn for refit.
- The [official history](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/UN/UK/UK-Med-II/UK-Med-2-8.html) says the *Tiger* convoy reached Alexandria on 12 May with more tanks, but unloading, desert modification and training delayed their use. The game does **not** grant those tanks immediately. It still lacks separate vehicle serviceability, transport borrowing, a detailed three-column command plan and the historical frontier anti-tank positions.

The scenario JSON copies owners and old placements solely to satisfy the structural checker. Campaign handoff retains the player's actual position and stocks. Neither chapter asserts a verified 5 or 15 May independent opening. Before promotion, research the actual frontier deployment and group strengths, model tank serviceability and anti-tank defences, test plausible human outcomes for both sides and review whether two-day preparation turns hide meaningful decisions.
