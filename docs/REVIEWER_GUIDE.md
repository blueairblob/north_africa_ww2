BENGHAZI HANDICAP — FIRST REVIEW BUILD

1. Extract the whole ZIP to a folder. Double-click Benghazi Handicap.exe inside the Benghazi Handicap folder. Keep the _internal folder beside the EXE.
2. Choose New battle for Sidi Rezegh or Operation Crusader. Choose Campaign research trial for a linked game from 1 September 1940 through 30 November 1942. Pick who commands each side: Human, Easy/Standard/Hard computer, or No orders. You can also watch two computer commanders play.
3. Click History during setup for sourced historical opening context. The Game menu's Historical context shows the same record during or after play, including what actually happened in the historical campaign. This is separate from your game result. Click How to play for the first-turn guide. F1 reopens it during play. Escape opens the Game menu. The window X saves and exits.
   Open Layers > Mission (or press F2) to see your High Command orders and bright green map targets. In Sidi Rezegh, both named places must be occupied and the marked corridor to Tobruk must be free of Axis units at the end.
4. Continue saved game resumes the newest save. F5 saves manually, F9 opens the save list, and F12 makes a screenshot. Options keeps your counter, sound and display preferences, shows the Game Files path, and opens that folder in Windows Explorer.

During the campaign research trial, press F6 or choose Game > Advance 5 turns to carry standing orders forward through a quiet stretch. Each day or two-day turn still resolves normally and is autosaved. The advance stops early if an enemy appears, a field event or arrival occurs, a place changes hands, a mission target is reached, a standing order ends, or your formation's strength, supply or status changes. Review the new position and give orders again. It is unavailable when a one-turn order is waiting or an enemy is already in sight.

The campaign research trial opens on 1 September 1940. Each result offers Continue campaign through the Italian advance, Compass, Tobruk, Crusader, the 1942 Axis return, Gazala, First Alamein, Alam Halfa, Second Alamein and the November pursuit. It ends on 30 November 1942 with a strategic campaign assessment. Played losses, positions, supply and fortifications carry forward; historical captures are never imposed. The 9th Australian Division returns in July under its existing formation IDs only if withdrawn and surviving. Many strengths, readiness dates, placements, repairs, minefields and supply flows remain estimates or incomplete. The separate Crusader battle is an independent historical start. Read CAMPAIGN RESEARCH NOTES.txt for evidence and limits. The Compass Research Trial.cmd remains an alternate shortcut to the December Compass chapter.

Campaign Research Trial.cmd is a shortcut to the September start against Hard Axis. Compass Research Trial.cmd still opens the December chapter directly. Historical Research\HISTORICAL STORY.md gives sourced before-and-after context for each campaign period and links to its detailed research notes; its outcome is separate from your played result. HISTORICAL VALIDATION LEDGER.txt identifies dated evidence, corrected deployments and incomplete 1942 forces.

Your files are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Benghazi Handicap\ on Windows:
  saves\         Saved games, including an autosave on exit and after turns
  screenshots\   F12 captures
  preferences.json
  logs\          Only when launched with --log for diagnosis

For feedback, please include the scenario, side, turn, what you expected and what happened. An F12 screenshot and a matching save file are especially helpful. This build is for reviewing playability and presentation; rules, scenario balance, artwork and opponent choices remain open to revision.

No installation is required. To remove the build, delete its extracted folder; your saved games remain in the app-data folder until you remove them separately.
This preview EXE is unsigned, so Windows may ask you to confirm that you trust it before the first launch.
