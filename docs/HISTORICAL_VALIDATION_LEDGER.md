# Historical validation ledger — North Africa research campaign

**Status, 12 November 2026:** chronological continuity is tested; historical simulation is **not validated**. This ledger distinguishes a dated source fact from a game entry date, strength or mechanism inferred to make the open campaign playable. A source saying that a formation was present at a later battle does not establish its earlier location, strength or serviceability.

The opening Compass roster, Italian camps and stocks still need a full 1 September order-of-battle audit. |

Their exact game strengths and entry dates are estimates. Counters of the 6th Australian Division, already called away for Greece, leave the active roster at the September chapter boundary while remaining in the save archive. Relief, refit and shipping still need source-led checks. |

## Validation gates

1. Reconstruct the September 1940 opening and November 1941, May 1942 and October 1942 orders of battle from dated primary records. Record each formation's actual location, manpower, tanks and guns separately from game-size conversions. Verify remaining redesignations and retire formations from the active roster when they left the theatre, without reviving destroyed units or erasing counterfactual survivors.
2. Model tank serviceability and repair, minefields and deliberate breaches, supply delivery through ports and roads, and surrender after isolation. Then test those mechanics against the documented Compass, Gazala and Alamein sequences and against plausible counterfactual play.
3. Run source-backed reference traces and human trials for both sides. Compare dated front lines, attrition, supply, and mission judgements, then revise chapter aims and computer decisions. A 33-chapter computer trace proves continuity only.
4. Keep **New campaign** gated until these checks pass. **Campaign research trial** remains the honest playable label. Historical milestones may appear in briefings, but captures and retreats must depend on the orders and actual battle state.
