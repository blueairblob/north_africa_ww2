# End-to-end North Africa campaign research trial

**Playable span:** 1 September 1940–30 November 1942, through 33 linked chapters. Start from **Campaign research trial** on the title screen. The separate Crusader and Sidi Rezegh battles remain independent starts. This is an end-to-end **research trial**, not a historically validated simulation or the normal New campaign release.

The [historical validation ledger](HISTORICAL_VALIDATION_LEDGER.md) records source-backed dates, corrected deployment errors and the remaining force and rules gaps.
The [historical story archive](HISTORICAL_STORY.md) preserves sourced before-and-after context for all 33 linked chapters, with a readable review copy and links to the detailed research notes. Its historical outcomes are reference material, never results imposed on the played campaign.

Each chapter hands the actual played units, control, stocks and fortifications to the next. Its historical title and dates set a briefing and dated force events; they do not award captures, force retreats or reset a counterfactual front. Battle turns are daily and most preparation periods use two-day turns. F6 advances at most five quiet turns before returning control when a decision matters. Save and resume retain the campaign history.

The added corps and division counters are aggregates with estimated strengths and entries. The final report judges strategic holdings across Egypt, Tobruk and the frontier and reports mission completion, surviving steps and stocks. |

## Verification and remaining work

The structural checker requires every chapter to meet the next on an exact date, all carried active units to retain ID and type, and all new units and dated events to have a source code. A deterministic Hard-versus-Hard trace completed all 33 chapters from 1 September 1940 through 30 November 1942 with invariant checks enabled. Its counterfactual final line left the Commonwealth in Egypt and Axis forces in Tobruk and Benghazi. This verifies continuity, **not** historical balance or a credible wartime outcome. The 1942 force structure, exact unit strengths, repairs, fuel and shipping, minefields and fortified boxes, surrender behaviour and air operations need source-led implementation and human review before the normal New campaign button is enabled.

The trial's final strategic verdict is a **game judgement**: control of Alexandria, El Alamein, Tobruk, the Egyptian frontier and Benghazi matters more than the last chapter's point margin. It cannot claim to recreate the actual 1942 outcome or award a historical medal. The menu labels it as a research trial for that reason.
