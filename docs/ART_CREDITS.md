# Formation sign image credits

The nine archival period photographs in `art/history` have captions, archive
credits, Commons file links and reuse status in the `image` entries of
[the period record](../data/history/north_africa_1940_1942.json). They are
also credited beside the images in the [review copy](HISTORICAL_STORY_REVIEW.md).

The following side-panel images come from Wikimedia Commons files used on
Wikipedia formation pages. They are displayed at reduced size in the game.
The original JPEG and PNG files for XXX Corps and 7th Armoured are stored
unchanged. The other PNGs are Wikimedia's own 500-pixel renders of the linked
SVG files, downloaded without further edits.
